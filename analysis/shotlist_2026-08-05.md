# Field shot list — 128 images, 2026-08-05 (summer, one day)

Covers what is capturable today: sun/shade, altitude, occlusion, surface,
buggy perspective, negatives, and a night LED block after dark. Rain, snow,
winter and fog are not available today — see issues #7–#11.

## Before anything: downwash check (not counted)

Hover the Neo 2 at 2 m then 1 m for 30 s over a plot seeded with 10 cigarette
butts and 2 wrappers. Photograph the layout before and after.

**If the prop wash moves the objects, every low drone shot below is compromised**
— switch those blocks to handheld-at-height and note it. Do this first.

## Kit

- Neo 2, batteries charged, 4K / 60 fps stills-from-video or photo mode
- ~40 cigarette butts (mixed: fresh, weathered, crushed, burnt, filter-up/down)
- 3 bottles, 3 cans, 5 wrappers, 2 plastic bags, 2 paper items
- A **scale reference** — a coloured card or ruler of known size, in frame
- Confusers: pine cones, stones, twigs, bark, dead leaves
- Notebook or voice memo for altitude / angle / surface / lighting per shot

---

## Block A — Altitude ladders · 35 images

**The highest-value block.** Place a scene, then shoot it from every height
**without moving anything**. Include the scale reference in every frame.

5 ladders × 7 heights = 35 images. Heights: **0.2, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 12 m**.

| # | Ladder | Surface | Scene contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 1–7 | Gravel | 5 butts spread ~30 cm apart + 1 can |
| A2 | 8–14 | Grass (tall) | 5 butts partly in grass + 1 bottle |
| A3 | 15–21 | Mown lawn | 5 butts + 1 wrapper |
| A4 | 22–28 | Asphalt / paving | 5 butts + 1 bag |
| A5 | 29–35 | Bare soil | 5 butts + mixed wrapper/paper |

Nadir at every height. Note the altitude on every frame — this block is
worthless without it.

## Block B — Cigarette butts at detection altitude · 18 images

Drone at **1.5–2 m, nadir**. This is the pipeline's critical altitude
(butts ≈ 22 px). Vary the butts themselves, not just the background.

| # | Content |
|---|---|
| 36–39 | Single butt, isolated, 4 different surfaces |
| 40–43 | Butt appearance: fresh / weathered / crushed / burnt |
| 44–46 | Filter-up, filter-down, on edge |
| 47–49 | Clusters: 3, 6, 12 butts in frame |
| 50–52 | Butts + other litter in same frame |
| 53 | Wet butt (wet one deliberately) |

## Block C — Sun, shade and dappled light · 14 images

Shoot **midday (12:00–14:00)** for maximum contrast, then a golden-hour subset
around 20:00.

| # | Content |
|---|---|
| 54–57 | Same 4 scenes in **full direct sun** |
| 58–61 | The **same 4 scenes in deep shadow** (move to shade, keep layout) |
| 62–64 | Dappled shade under a tree — alternating light/dark across the object |
| 65–66 | Backlit / into the sun, with lens flare |
| 67 | Object shadow that mimics a butt shape |

Pairs 54–57 vs 58–61 must be the same layout — that is what makes them useful.

## Block D — Occlusion ladder · 16 images

Same object, progressively more hidden. 4 occluders × 4 levels.

| # | Occluder | Levels |
|---|---|---|
| 68–71 | Tall grass | none / light / partial / heavy |
| 72–75 | Dead leaves | none / light / partial / heavy |
| 76–79 | Loose soil / gravel | none / light / partial / heavy |
| 80–83 | Other litter overlapping | none / light / partial / heavy |

Levels: none = fully visible, light <25 %, partial 25–60 %, heavy >60 % hidden.
**Record the level per shot.**

## Block F — Buggy perspective and oblique angles · 13 images

Handheld or on the buggy. This is the retrieval stage's geometry.

| # | Content |
|---|---|
| 96–99 | 0.2 m, oblique 0–30°, 4 objects |
| 100–102 | 1 m nadir and 1 m at 45° |
| 103–106 | Angle sweep on one fixed object: 15°, 30°, 45°, 60° |
| 107–108 | Azimuth: same object from opposite sides (sun behind / in front) |

## Block G — Negatives and confusers · 8 images

Precision is the priority — these directly reduce wasted buggy trips.

| # | Content |
|---|---|
| 109–111 | Verified-empty ground: gravel, grass, asphalt |
| 112–114 | Confusers only, no litter: pine cones / twigs+bark / dead leaves |
| 115 | Cigarette-sized sticks on gravel (worst confuser) |
| 116 | Natural debris field, zero litter |

## Block H — Night with LED · 12 images

After ~22:00. Sunset ≈ 21:15, so full dark from about 22:00.

| # | Content |
|---|---|
| 117–119 | **Omnidirectional** LED: butts at 0.2, 1, 2 m |
| 120–122 | **Directed** LED: same three heights — note hotspot and falloff |
| 123–124 | Directed LED at a shallow angle: long hard shadows |
| 125–126 | **Wet surface** under LED — specular glare (wet a patch deliberately) |
| 127 | Object at the edge of the light pool, half-lit |
| 128 | Dusk / twilight transition, no artificial light |

Block H is worth the late night: night is a stated project goal with zero
existing coverage, and wet-surface glare (125–126) is the hardest case in it.

## Record for every image

Altitude · camera angle · surface · lighting · occlusion level · artificial
light yes/no. Without this the images cannot be split by condition later, and
the whole point of a structured capture is lost.
